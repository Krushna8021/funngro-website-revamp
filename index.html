QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Company FAQs

What is Funngro?

Funngro describes itself as a platform connecting young people with companies and project opportunities. Visit its official website for the latest details.

How should a company get started?

Use the official Funngro website and follow the current company registration or contact process shown there.

What should a good project brief include?

Include the objective, scope, deliverables, deadline, required skills, review process and contact person. Avoid sharing confidential data unnecessarily.

Does this demo collect company information?

No. This two-page concept is a static front-end demo and does not submit or store forms.
