QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Teen FAQs

What can I learn through projects?

Depending on available opportunities, projects may help you practise creativity, communication, research, digital tools and other practical skills.

Will every project guarantee earnings?

No. Read each opportunity's eligibility, acceptance, completion and reward conditions carefully. Never assume payment is guaranteed before checking the terms.

How do I choose a safe project?

Use official platform channels, check the task details, avoid upfront payments and never share your password or OTP. Ask a parent or guardian for help when needed.

Does this demo accept applications?

No. This is a static design concept and does not collect personal information or process applications.
